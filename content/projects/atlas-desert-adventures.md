## Overview

Atlas Desert Adventures presents curated experiences around Morocco through localized tour content and straightforward navigation. Its pages showcase desert tours, mountain treks, and day trips.

## What the project includes

- Six-language website structure, including Arabic with right-to-left support.
- Dedicated itinerary and destination content for tour discovery.
- Localized SEO metadata, sitemap, robots settings, and social previews.

## Technical approach

Built with Next.js App Router and `next-intl`, the project focuses on translation-aware routing, accessible content discovery, and search-friendly pages. Styling mixes Tailwind CSS and custom CSS with restrained motion.

## Scope and status

The repository includes tour program descriptions and inquiry contact details. This case study does not claim measured booking results.
