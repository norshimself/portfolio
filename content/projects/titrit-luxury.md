## Overview

Titrit Luxury focuses on presenting desert stays and curated travel experiences with immersive imagery and clear ways to explore offerings. Visitors can browse tours, camps, and practical transport options.

## What the project includes

- Localized destination, tour, bivouac, gallery, and transportation routes.
- Hero booking widget and enquiry-oriented page elements.
- Gallery, map, and travel content components supporting exploration.

## Technical approach

The frontend uses Next.js with `next-intl` for localized pages, Chakra UI for components, Framer Motion for transitions, and Leaflet for map-oriented interfaces.

## Scope and status

This is a travel and hospitality website showcase. No unsupported performance, booking, or revenue outcomes are included.
