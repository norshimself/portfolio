## Overview

Seven24 Market is a grocery-focused online shopping experience for customers in Morocco. The repository covers both the customer-facing store and internal catalog and order-management pages.

## What the project includes

- Browseable product categories, weekly deals, and featured new arrivals.
- Product details, basket, checkout, wishlist, and account screens.
- Administrative pages for products, customers, orders, and store settings.

## Technical approach

Next.js and TypeScript provide the storefront and administrative routes in one application. The project uses PostgreSQL and Drizzle ORM and includes authentication and validation libraries for account and checkout flows.

## Scope and status

The repository demonstrates end-to-end commerce route design. No sales, traffic, or conversion statistics have been added to this case study.
