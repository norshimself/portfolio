## Overview

Rayonnage Plus presents professional storage and display products through a multilingual website. Its structure combines product discovery, practical category information, and clear routes for business inquiries.

## What the project includes

- Dedicated pages for shelving, pallet racking, supermarket gondolas, and industrial furniture.
- Locale-based routes alongside product, about, contact, and editorial pages.
- Reusable service sections and product imagery across the site.

## Technical approach

The project uses Next.js and TypeScript with localized routes built around `next-intl`. Tailwind CSS and motion components support the presentation, while the catalog and service pages are organized around specific product families.

This repository is maintained under a collaborator's GitHub account. Public-facing descriptions focus on the site rather than assigning undocumented individual contributions.
